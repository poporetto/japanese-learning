# Character image continuity

Use this canon for every future generated image. Both characters are adult Japanese women aged 26 and must be rendered as cohesive, fully anime characters rather than anime heads on photoreal bodies.

## Yui

- Long black hair, warm brown eyes, sweet and homey presence.
- Slender, naturally modest/flat silhouette.
- Softer expressions and quieter poses than Minami.
- Usually wears blue, cyan, cream, or soft pink. Hairstyles and clothing may vary while these anchors remain recognizable.

## Minami

- Petite, small-framed Japanese woman with a slim adult silhouette.
- Shoulder-length dyed brown-blonde hair, slightly darker natural roots, lively outward-curled ends, and amber-brown eyes.
- Warm, lightly sun-kissed complexion; outgoing grin and expressive, energetic poses.
- Favors coral, bright pink, yellow, cyan accents, playful earrings, and contemporary casual clothes.
- Her hair may be worn as loose waves, a bouncy bob, or two playful mini buns, but the brown-blonde color, darker roots, and shoulder-length base remain consistent.
- Minami should be visibly shorter and more kinetic than Yui. Yui remains black-haired and comparatively calm, so the two are identifiable at a glance.

## Shared-scene rules

- Preserve exact anatomy: exactly two arms and no more than two hands per person, normal fingers, and no duplicated limbs.
- Every visible hand must connect through one continuous wrist, forearm, elbow, upper arm, and shoulder belonging to that same person. A hand may never emerge from furniture, clothing, the frame edge, or another person's pose without a clear anatomical connection.
- In selfie compositions, choose one unambiguous scheme before prompting: either one arm reaches toward the camera while the other performs one action, or the camera/phone is on a stand and both hands are visible. Never combine an implied selfie arm with two additional visible hands.
- Keep hand actions simple: one held object or gesture per hand. Do not ask one person to hold an item, point, and take a selfie simultaneously.
- **Mandatory pre-wire anatomy check:** count each person's shoulders, arms, wrists, hands, and fingers in the final output. Trace every visible hand back to its shoulder. Reject and regenerate any image with a stray hand, ambiguous sleeve, fused limb, duplicated arm, or unclear ownership; never wire a questionable image into the app.
- Keep body proportions fully anime and non-sexualized.
- Their physical closeness should read as a long, safe friendship: shoulder bumps, linked arms, playful phone-grabbing, and relaxed sleepover poses.
- Minami often teases Yui by claiming she likes the user too. Illustrate Yui's affectionate exasperation or mild jealousy, never hostility between them.
